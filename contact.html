How can we help?

Questions,
answered.

How do I donate to a specific campaign?
Visit the Campaigns page, choose the cause that speaks to you and use the donate button on that card. You can give once or set up a monthly gift.
Can I volunteer without prior experience?
Absolutely. We offer orientation and pair new volunteers with experienced team leads. Bring your time and curiosity — we'll handle the rest.
How is my donation used?
94% of every rupee goes directly to programs in the field. The remainder supports the systems that keep our work accountable and sustainable.
Can my organization partner with NGO Charity?
Yes. We work with companies, schools and community groups. Email partners@ngocharity.org and our partnerships team will reach out.
Do you share updates on campaign progress?
We publish progress reports on every active campaign, with honest updates on what is working and what we are learning.
