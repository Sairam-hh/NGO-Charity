We believe change is a practice.

For 18 years, NGO Charity has worked with communities to turn resources into agency, and good intentions into useful action.

Our story

Start close.
Stay curious.

We began in 2008 with one after-school circle and a conviction that the people closest to a challenge should help design the answer.

Today, our teams work across education, health, nourishment and livelihoods — not as heroes, but as committed partners.
